Clips joined with short padding → one sentence of audio
  • Explicit spans: you can see and test what was detected
  • Hebrew reaches the voice in Hebrew script, with niqqud
  • Fails safe: a missed word is read as English, as today
  • Cost: a split, an extra call and an audio seam per Hebrew span

  • No seams, one call

  • No handle to fix one word, and no way to test what it decided
